Treatments at the clinic

Prices depend on what the check-up finds, so ask for one when you book.

  • Check-up and cleaning

    We look at every tooth and gum line, then scale and polish.

  • Root canal treatment

    Cleans out an infected nerve so the tooth can stay.

  • Braces and clear aligners

    Metal or ceramic braces, or clear trays you take out to eat.

  • Crowns and bridges

    A cap for a broken tooth, or a bridge across a gap.

  • Implants and dentures

    Fixed or removable replacements for missing teeth.

Your first visit

From the first question to the next check-up date, in four parts.

  1. We listen first

    Pain, a chipped tooth or a routine check: tell us what brings you in, then we look.

  2. An X-ray when it is needed

    It shows what the eye cannot: decay between teeth, the roots and the bone around them.

  3. A plan before any work

    We explain the options and what each one costs. Nothing starts until you choose.

  4. Treatment and a recall date

    We do the work you agreed to, then tell you when to come back for the next check-up.
